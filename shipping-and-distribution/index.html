Bring Your Own Account (BYOA) for Ecommerce Shipping

Keep Your Shipping. Upgrade Your Fulfilment.

Why Businesses Choose BYOA

Keep Your Negotiated Shipping Rates

Continue using your existing courier contracts or shipping platform without mandatory markups or hidden fees.

Complete Ownership

Your shipping accounts, courier relationships, negotiated rates, contracts and shipment history remain yours.

No Migration. No Disruption.

Avoid rebuilding shipping workflows or opening new courier accounts. Connect your existing platform and continue fulfilling orders without interruption.

Multi-Carrier Flexibility

Choose the right carrier for every shipment. Use express couriers, economical services, regional carriers or specialised providers—all through your existing account.

Automated Shipping Operations

WareVolt automatically generates labels, AWB numbers, manifests and dispatch documentation while synchronising information across your systems.

Real-Time Shipment Synchronisation

Tracking numbers, delivery milestones and shipment status updates are synchronised across your shipping platform, sales channels and WareVolt dashboard.

MANAGED FREIGHT NETWORK

Nationwide Freight for Bulk Distribution

Not every shipment is a parcel. Moving pallets, wholesale inventory, retail replenishment stock, manufacturing goods or inter-warehouse transfers requires a dedicated freight solution.

WareVolt's Managed Freight Network connects you with trusted transport partners across India, providing reliable FTL, PTL and LTL services. From booking and documentation to carrier coordination and delivery, we manage every stage of the freight journey so your inventory moves efficiently and on schedule.

Freight Services

Part Truckload (PTL)

Cost-effective medium shipments with shared vehicle space.

Less Than Truckload (LTL)

Retail & Wholesale Distribution

Inter-Warehouse Transfers
