Analytics & Reporting

WareVolt Scale™ brings order, inventory, warehouse, shipping, and returns data into a single operational view — giving leadership the metrics to run the business proactively, not reactively.

Speak to an expert

Real-Time Operational Intelligence. Complete Visibility.

Supply chains generate millions of discrete data points every single day. WareVolt Scale™ aggregates, normalizes, and visualizes this data into unified operational intelligence—giving your team instant clarity over order cycles, carrier SLA delivery, and warehouse throughput.

Identify delivery bottlenecks before they hurt customer reviews, measure true unit fulfillment economics, and streamline cross-facility operations with confidence backed by verifiable real-time analytics.

Key Capabilities

Full-spectrum fulfillment analytics engineered for high-growth commerce brands.

Real-Time Executive Dashboards

Monitor orders dispatched, fulfillment velocity, order aging, and delivery SLA compliance through intuitive visual dashboards.

Multi-Carrier SLA Benchmarking

Measure carrier transit times, on-time delivery rates, exception rates, and cost per kilogram across zones in real time.

Inventory Health & Aging Reports

Track SKU turnover ratios, slow-moving items, deadstock risks, and days-of-inventory on hand to optimize capital allocation.

Fulfillment Cost & Unit Economics

Break down storage, pick-pack, handling, packaging, and freight costs per order to safeguard gross margins.

Reverse Logistics & RTO Analytics

Uncover return patterns, customer return reasons, non-delivery reports (NDR), and transit damage metrics to minimize returns.

Labor & Warehouse Productivity

Analyze picks per hour, dock-to-stock turnaround times, packing speed, and shift-level throughput across fulfillment centers.

Demand Forecasting & Velocity

Utilize historical trends and seasonal velocity forecasting to project reorder quantities and prevent costly stockouts.

Custom Report Builder & Data Feeds

Export scheduled reports in CSV/Excel, configure automated email alerts, or stream live data via webhooks and BI connectors.

Enterprise Intelligence & BI

Actionable Insights Across Every Supply Chain Touchpoint.

From floor-level warehouse supervisors to executive leadership, WareVolt Analytics connects disparate data streams across sales channels, WMS workflows, and last-mile logistics into a single source of truth. Schedule automated reports, configure proactive alerts, and feed clean fulfillment data into your enterprise BI stack effortlessly.
